# Stage 2 — Sectra AB Company Selection Memo (Revised)

**Re:** Selection of Sectra AB (publ) for BUS 629 Accounting Ratios Project

## Executive Summary

I have selected **Sectra AB (publ)** (SECT B, Nasdaq Stockholm Large Cap) — a Swedish medical-imaging IT and cybersecurity company — for the BUS 629 ratios project. Sectra is a non-U.S. IFRS reporter mid-transition from on-premise PACS licensing to cloud-native SaaS delivery, with FY2024/2025 net sales of SEK 3,239.8M (+9.3% YoY) and underlying operating margin of 18.9%. It satisfies the four eligibility criteria, offers three distinct ratio-analysis angles (profitability disruption, working-capital reshape, currency translation), and aligns with my professional focus on Digital Health.

---

## Background

Sectra (founded 1978, Linköping, Sweden) operates three segments: Imaging IT Solutions (dominant — PACS, RIS, AI-enabled imaging), Secure Communications (encrypted defense/government systems), and Business Innovation (orthopedic, oncology). The U.S. is its largest market; KLAS Research has ranked Sectra #1 in PACS customer satisfaction for 13 consecutive years.

**Selection rationale:** *(a) Industry alignment* — my 23-year career in Vietnam's medical-equipment sector and VP role at the HCMC Medical Equipment Association make Sectra's PACS-to-SaaS transition directly relevant to my Digital Health work. *(b) Analytical richness* — the revenue-model shift reshapes profitability, working capital, and cash flow simultaneously; a SEK 110M non-recurring patent settlement in FY24/25 requires reported-versus-underlying adjustment. *(c) International finance dimension* — SEK reporting against majority-non-SEK revenue makes currency translation a live variable.

---

## Method

Primary data: Sectra's IFRS-audited Annual Reports FY22/23–FY24/25 plus the FY24/25 year-end press release (investor.sectra.com). Comparables: ASX-listed Pro Medicus as the pure-play SaaS PACS benchmark. FX data: Riksbank.se. Three IFRS-specific considerations apply: (i) Sectra's fiscal year ends April 30 (offset from calendar-year peers); (ii) by-nature income-statement presentation requires care on gross-margin and inventory-turnover interpretation; (iii) the SEK 110M patent settlement will be stripped to compute underlying operating profit (SEK 613M underlying vs SEK 723M reported).

---

## Findings — Preliminary Hypotheses

**H1 (Working Capital).** DSO will lengthen 15–25 days FY23/24 → FY24/25 and deferred revenue will expand 25–40% **because** the SaaS transition shifts revenue-recognition timing — large multi-year cloud contracts generate upfront cash collections recognized as deferred revenue while concurrent on-premise revenue declines tighten receivables turnover.

**H2 (Profitability).** Underlying operating margin will compress 100–200 bps FY24/25 → FY25/26 (from 18.9% toward 17.0–18.0%) **because** (a) high-margin on-premise license recognition is decreasing and (b) upfront SaaS delivery costs precede revenue. CEO Kronander explicitly guided this pressure in the FY24/25 year-end commentary.

**H3 (Cash Conversion & FX).** Reported SEK revenue growth will lag underlying constant-currency growth by 100–150 bps and CFO/Net Income will remain above 1.5x **because** prior-year FX tailwinds have reversed and SaaS prepayment dynamics structurally elevate operating cash flow above accrual earnings — already confirmed at 1.64× in FY24/25 (CFO SEK 922.4M / NI SEK 563.4M).

---

## Implications — Ratio Categories Preview

The analytically meaningful categories are **profitability** (operating margin reported and adjusted; ROE; ROIC), **working-capital efficiency** (DSO, deferred-revenue dynamics, cash conversion cycle), and **cash flow strength** (OCF/NI, FCF margin). Leverage ratios are de-prioritized — minimal long-term debt and AGM 2025's extraordinary dividend signal low capital-structure risk.

---

## Limitations & Next Steps

Hypotheses rest on FY24/25 disclosures and forward CEO guidance; Q1 FY25/26 results (Sep 2025) will refine the trajectory. The pending Oxipit acquisition and Paige.AI partnership introduce M&A and intangibles considerations for Stage 3 follow-up.
